# 🚗 Car Sales Dashboard | Tableau

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Car Sales Dashboard** is an interactive data visualization project built using Tableau to analyze car sales performance from **October 2, 2020, to December 31, 2021**.

The dashboard provides insights into total sales, average car prices, cars sold, weekly sales trends, body-style preferences, color-wise sales distribution, dealer-region performance, and company-wise sales.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall car sales performance.
- Track year-to-date (YTD) sales and average selling prices.
- Understand weekly sales trends and fluctuations.
- Compare sales across different car body styles and colors.
- Evaluate dealer-region performance by transmission type.
- Compare car companies based on sales, units sold, and average price.
- Provide interactive filters for exploring specific segments of the data.

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. YTD Sales Weekly Trends
- Visualizes weekly sales performance.
- Helps identify sales peaks, fluctuations, and trends over time.

### 2. YTD Total Sales by Body Style
- Compares sales across different body styles, including SUV, Sedan, Hatchback, Passenger, and Hardtop.
- Helps identify body styles contributing to total revenue.

### 3. YTD Total Sales by Color
- Displays the distribution of sales across car colors.
- Helps compare the contribution of different color categories.

### 4. YTD Cars Sold by Dealer Region
- Compares car sales across dealer regions.
- Uses stacked bars to distinguish between manual and automatic transmissions.

### 5. Company-wise Sales Trend
- Presents company-level comparisons using cars sold, average price, total sales, and percentage contribution to YTD sales.
- Helps identify leading companies and compare their performance.

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes filters for:
- Date
- Engine
- Body Style
- Gender
- Week of Date
- Transmission

These filters allow users to explore the data according to different time periods and vehicle characteristics.
